## eliot-general

The eliot-general (@nongnu.org) mailing-list is designed for users who want to ask some questions, make suggestions, report bugs or, more generally, for any discussion concerning Eliot. A notification is also sent to the list for each new release of Eliot.

You can subscribe (or unsubscribe) on this page: [http://lists.nongnu.org/mailman/listinfo/eliot-general](http://lists.nongnu.org/mailman/listinfo/eliot-general). You can also [send an email](mailto:eliot-general@nongnu.org) without being subscribed, but your message will not appear until a moderator approves it.

## eliot-dev

The eliot-dev (@nongnu.org) mailing-list is used for the development of Eliot. Subscribers receive a commit message every time a change is made to the source code. This list is the right place to ask for help about compilation, to send patches, etc.

You can subscribe or unsubscribe on [http://lists.nongnu.org/mailman/listinfo/eliot-dev](http://lists.nongnu.org/mailman/listinfo/eliot-dev).
