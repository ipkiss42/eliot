## Other Scrabble tools {#scrabble}

- [NASPA Zyzzyva](https://www.scrabbleplayers.org/w/NASPA_Zyzzyva:_The_Last_Word_in_Word_Study): word study and word checking.
- [Internet Scrabble Club](https://isc.ro/): online Scrabble in several languages.
- [Quackle](https://www.quackle.org/): a tool for analysing classic games and playing against the computer.
- [Scrabble3D](https://github.com/Scrabble3D): Scrabble on classic or three-dimensional boards.

## Technical reading {#technical-links}

- [DAWG data structure](https://www.wutka.com/dawg.html): the dictionary data structure used in Eliot.
- [The World's Fastest Scrabble Program](/download/aj.pdf) (PDF) — Andrew W. Appel and Guy J. Jacobson, 1988. The move-generation algorithm used in Eliot.
- [A Faster Scrabble Move Generation Algorithm](https://ericsink.com/downloads/faster-scrabble-gordon.pdf) (PDF) — Steven A. Gordon, 1994. The GADDAG move-generation algorithm.
- [Qt documentation](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/): documentation for the framework used by Eliot's graphical interface.
