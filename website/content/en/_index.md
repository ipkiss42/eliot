modes:
- >-
  **Classic** (called *Free game* in Eliot): take turns playing with your own rack.
  Ideal for games with friends or family.
- >-
  **Duplicate**: everyone uses the same rack and board. Each player scores their own word,
  and the best move is placed on the board. Common in clubs and competitions.
- >-
  **Training**: practise alone, choose your rack and explore possible moves to improve at duplicate.
- >-
  **Arbitration**: manage a duplicate game for a club or competition, recording players' moves
  and choosing the move played on the shared board.
- >-
  **Topping**: find the highest-scoring move as quickly as possible. You can try multiple moves
  and request hints, which incur time penalties. A challenge for experienced players.

## Free Scrabble game

Eliot is an [open source](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_source) Scrabble game, freely available under the [GNU General Public License (GPL), version 2 or later](https://github.com/ipkiss42/eliot/blob/main/COPYING).

Eliot also provides many tools for advanced **search** such as the following:

- Search for the best move on a given board and rack. This search is almost instantaneous!
- Search for the possible connections, "Benjamins" (addition of three letters at the beginning of a previous word), or for all the words that can be composed with the letters of the rack.
- You can also use [regular expressions](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_expression) to search in the dictionary.
